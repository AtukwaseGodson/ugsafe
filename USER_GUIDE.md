# UgSafe AI: User Guide & Emergency Manual

Welcome to **UgSafe**, your AI-powered companion designed to provide instant assistance during road accidents and fire emergencies. This guide will help you set up and use the app effectively, even if you are a stranger to your current location.

---

## 🚀 1. Quick Setup (Crucial for Emergencies)

To ensure the app is ready when every second counts, please perform these two steps immediately:

### A. Add the Quick Access Tile
1. Pull down your phone's **Quick Settings** (notification panel) twice.
2. Tap the **Edit (pencil)** icon.
3. Find the **"UgSafe Scan"** tile and drag it into your active tiles.
4. **Why?** This allows you to launch the emergency scanner with one tap, even from your **lock screen**.

### B. Grant Permissions
When you first open the app, it will ask for:
- **Camera:** To analyze the scene for accidents or fire.
- **Location:** To find the nearest help and send your coordinates to authorities.
- **Notifications:** To keep the "Instant Scan" shortcut active in your tray.

---

## 📸 2. Using the AI Scanner

1. **Open the App:** Use the Quick Settings tile or the app icon.
2. **Scan the Scene:** Tap the large **"SCAN NOW"** button or the center camera box.
3. **AI Analysis:** The app will take a photo and analyze it locally. 
   - **Accidents:** Detects vehicle crashes or road incidents.
   - **Fire:** Detects active flames or smoke hazards.
4. **Safety Status:** The app will display a green "SAFETY STATUS" if clear, or a red "ACTION REQUIRED" if a threat is found.

---

## 🚑 3. Emergency Actions

If the AI detects an emergency, UgSafe automatically takes the following steps:

### A. Automatic Reporting
The app immediately prepares an anonymous report containing:
- The nature of the emergency.
- Your exact GPS coordinates.
- A photo of the scene.
- This is sent securely to the **Fire Brigade** or **Police Department** via encrypted channels.

### B. Stranger's Navigation Guide
If you are in an unfamiliar area:
1. Tap **"NAVIGATE TO NEAREST HOSPITAL/FIRE STATION"**.
2. This opens Google Maps with the **shortest, fastest route** already calculated from your current position.
3. Follow the turn-by-turn voice guidance to reach safety.

### C. Share Your Route
Tap **"Share Route"** to send a pre-filled emergency message and your live navigation link to family or friends.

### D. Manual 999 Dialing
A prominent **"Call 999"** button is always available at the bottom of the emergency screen for direct voice contact with emergency dispatchers.

---

## 🏥 4. Finding Nearby Services

Even if an emergency isn't detected, you can manually find help:
- **Hospital:** Maps the fastest route to the nearest medical facility.
- **Police:** Routes you to the nearest police station.
- **Fire:** Routes you to the nearest fire station.

---

## 🛡️ 5. Privacy & Safety Note

- **Anonymity:** Your reports are sent securely. We prioritize getting help to your location without compromising your personal identity.
- **AI Limitations:** While highly accurate, AI can make mistakes. **Always prioritize your personal safety** and use your own judgment. If you see danger, call 999 immediately regardless of what the screen says.
